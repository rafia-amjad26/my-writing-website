About the Story

Makhtoom is not just a story — it is the beginning of everything. It holds the first emotions I ever wrote, the raw thoughts, and the unfiltered truth of a writer discovering herself.

Though unfinished, it carries a depth that cannot be ignored — a silent intensity that reflects how stories are born from feelings we don't always understand.

“Some stories are not completed… they stay within you.”
